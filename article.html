EDITORIAL

The next chapter of Johannesburg's education story

JOZI 100 EDITORIAL DESK · 1 NOVEMBER 2026

Johannesburg is shaped every day in its classrooms, staff rooms, libraries, sports fields and school communities.

JOZI 100 Journal is beginning a city-wide editorial project to document the institutions, educators and learners contributing to that story. Our aim is to publish thoughtful profiles, useful information and carefully researched features that give communities a closer look at the work happening across the city.

A publication built on evidence and conversation

As the project grows, school profiles will be developed using verified information, direct engagement and clearly identified sources. We will distinguish editorial coverage from paid partnerships, and we will not present an unsupported ranking as an independent finding.

Schools interested in sharing their story can contact the editorial desk.
